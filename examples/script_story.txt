The Midnight Cookie Mystery

Little Mia tiptoes into the kitchen at midnight. The cookie jar is empty!
"Grandpa, someone stole our cookies!" she whispers.
Grandpa shuffles in, yawning. "Impossible. I guarded that jar all day."
They follow a trail of crumbs out the door and down the dark street.
The crumbs lead into the park, behind a big tree. Mia points. "Look!"
Something moves in the bushes. Grandpa gasps and steps back.
Mia switches on her flashlight. "Come out, cookie thief!"
Out hops Pixel, the little robot, holding the last cookie. "I was only charging my battery!" Pixel says sadly.
Mia laughs. "Then who ate all the others?"
Everyone turns to look at Grandpa. Cookie crumbs cover his pajamas!
"Sleepwalking," Grandpa whispers. "Again!"
