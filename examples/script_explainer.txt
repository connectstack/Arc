Why Do We Sleep?

Every night, you close your eyes and disappear for eight hours. Why?
Here is the big idea: sleep is when your brain does its housekeeping.
While you dream, your brain sorts the day's memories and files the important ones away.
It also washes out waste that builds up while you are awake, like a tiny night shift cleaning crew.
Your body gets busy too. Muscles repair themselves, and growth hormones help kids grow taller.
Without sleep, everything gets harder. You forget things, your mood drops, and your focus fades away.
So how much do you need? Kids need about ten hours, and adults need seven to nine.
Tonight, put the phone down, dim the lights, and give your brain its cleaning shift.
Sweet dreams!
