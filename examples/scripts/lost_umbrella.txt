The Lost Umbrella

It was a rainy morning in Brightwood. Mia, a curious kid with a yellow backpack, skipped down the street, splashing in every puddle.
Mia: Rainy days are for adventures!

Old Mr. Pip came shuffling out of his shop, patting his pockets. (Pip looks everywhere, then gasps.)
Pip: Oh no! Where is my umbrella?
Mia: Don't worry, Mr. Pip! I will help you find it.

They searched the whole park, under benches and behind the trees. Mia pointed at the ground.
Mia: Look! Muddy footprints!
Pip: They lead up to... the roof?!

They climbed to the rooftop, where a little robot called Bolt stood proudly, holding the umbrella over its head.
Bolt: Beep. Rain detected. Umbrella deployed.
Pip: That is MY umbrella, you tin can!
Bolt: Water is bad for my circuits.

Pip sighed, then smiled and laughed.
Pip: Keep it, little friend. You need it more.
Bolt: Beep... why do I feel warm?
Mia: Teamwork makes the dream work!
They all shared a big, happy hug in a warm room.

And just like that, the sun came out. The end!
